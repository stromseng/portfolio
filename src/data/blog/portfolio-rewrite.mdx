For a long time, this site used Sanity as a headless CMS. During my recent redesign, I swapped it for Astro's Content Collections.

Sanity was overkill; managing content locally is much smoother. I used Claude to script the migration to MDX, a few prompts later, it was done.

So far I really enjoy the experience. As I update this post, I really agree with the [recent post by Lee Robinson](https://leerob.com/agents), who migrated Cursor's documentation from a CMS to code and markdown. His reasons are quite similar to mine, although my main motivation was simplicity and ease of AI use.
